How It Works

Simple for parents, exciting for kids

1

Create Tasks

Parents set up tasks with point values. Daily chores, homework, or one-off jobs — you decide.

2

Complete & Approve

Children mark tasks as done. Parents review and approve to award the points.

3

Earn Points

Points stack up with every completed task. Watch progress grow on the dashboard.

4

Claim Rewards

Spend points on rewards set by parents — a movie night, a treat, or a special day out.
